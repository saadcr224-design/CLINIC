This Privacy Policy explains how {{clinic}} ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website, book appointments, or use the patient portal. We process personal data in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations issued by the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

1. Data we collect

  • Identity & contact data: name, National ID / Iqama number, date of birth, mobile number, email.
  • Health data: reason for visit, appointment history, lab reports and prescriptions (sensitive data).
  • Insurance data: insurer, policy and membership number, used to verify eligibility through NPHIES.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected for security and, with your consent, analytics.

2. Why we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you of appointments (SMS, WhatsApp, email).
  • To provide medical care and maintain your medical record as required by Ministry of Health regulations.
  • To process insurance claims and payments.
  • To secure our services, detect fraud and keep audit records.
  • To send health tips, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Legal basis

We rely on your consent, the performance of the healthcare service you request, and our legal obligations under Saudi health regulations. Sensitive health data is processed only for healthcare purposes and with appropriate safeguards.

4. Sharing

We share data only with your insurer (via NPHIES), licensed laboratories and service providers that act on our instructions (for example SMS and hosting providers) under written data-processing agreements, and with authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

5. Storage & transfers

Patient data is hosted within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any transfer outside the Kingdom follows the PDPL's cross-border transfer rules.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for staff, audit logging of every access to patient records, web application firewall protection and regular vulnerability testing.

7. Retention

Medical records are kept for the period required by Ministry of Health regulations. Enquiries and marketing data are deleted when no longer needed or when you withdraw consent.

8. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access and obtain a copy of your data, to correct it, to request its destruction where legally permitted, and to withdraw consent. Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@example-clinic.sa. You may also lodge a complaint with SDAIA.

9. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for sessions and security. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, with IP anonymisation) are used only where enabled.
